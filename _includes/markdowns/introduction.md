My name is Zach. My pronouns are he/him/his. I'm a senior research engineer at [MongoDB Research](https://www.mongodb.com/company/research) and a visiting researcher at [New York University](https://cims.nyu.edu/dynamic/) hosted by [Joseph Bonneau](https://jbonneau.com/).

My research vision is to improve the efficiency, interpretability, and overall security for privacy-preserving systems in real-world scenarios. To do this, I maintain a broad set of interests in cryptography, formal methods, data structures, computer systems, and human factors. My [recent work](#publications) focuses on leakage attacks and cryptanalysis, designing efficient encrypted data structures, and application-centric cryptographic design to support at-risk communities.

I completed a [concurrent Sc.B. and Sc.M.](notes/brown-courses/) at [Brown University](https://brown.edu), where my studies were generously supported by grants from [Brown CS](https://cs.brown.edu/news/2021/03/24/ross-briden-and-zachary-espiritu-win-randy-f-pausch-computer-science-undergraduate-summer-research-award/), [CrowdStrike](https://www.crowdstrike.org/scholarships.html), <a href="https://iamcybersafe.org/s/undergraduate-scholarships">(ISC)<sup>2</sup></a>, and the [CIT Group](https://www.cit.com/). I was also affiliated with the [Cloud Security Group](http://caps.cs.brown.edu/) with [Roberto Tamassia](https://www.tamassia.net/), the [Encrypted Systems Lab](http://esl.cs.brown.edu/) with [Seny Kamara](http://cs.brown.edu/~seny/), and [Brown PLT](https://cs.brown.edu/research/plt/) with [Shriram Krishnamurthi](http://cs.brown.edu/~sk/). I also spent a lot of time [teaching](#teaching); in particular, I was the course designer and Head Teaching Assistant of the Computer Science department’s flagship [computer systems security course](https://cs.brown.edu/courses/info/csci1660/) from 2019 to 2021.
